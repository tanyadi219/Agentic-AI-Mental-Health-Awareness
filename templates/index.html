5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

When overwhelmed, bring yourself back to now:

  • 👁️ Name 5 things you can see
  • ✋ Notice 4 things you can touch
  • 👂 Identify 3 things you can hear
  • 👃 Find 2 things you can smell
  • 👅 Notice 1 thing you can taste
💨

Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)

  • Inhale slowly for 4 seconds
  • Hold breath for 4 seconds
  • Exhale slowly for 4 seconds
  • Hold for 4 seconds

Repeat 4–6 cycles. Activates the parasympathetic nervous system and reduces cortisol.

Suicide Prevention Facts

  • Warning signs: talking about death, giving away possessions, social withdrawal, extreme mood swings.
  • Asking someone directly about suicide does not increase risk — it shows you care.
  • Say: "I hear you. You're not alone. I'm here."
  • Secure or remove access to means when safe to do so.
  • Stay with the person; don't leave them alone in a crisis.
🌱

Daily Self-Care Practices

  • Sleep 7–9 hours on a consistent schedule
  • Move your body — even a 10-minute walk helps
  • Eat nourishing food and stay hydrated
  • Limit alcohol and substance use
  • Journal or practice gratitude writing
  • Connect meaningfully with trusted people
  • Spend time in nature or natural light

ℹ️ About MindBridge

What is MindBridge?

MindBridge is an Agentic AI companion designed for mental health awareness and suicide prevention. It provides compassionate, non-judgmental emotional support and connects users with evidence-based coping strategies and crisis resources.

🤖 AI Model

MindBridge is powered by openai/gpt-oss-120b, an open-source 120B parameter language model running via the Groq inference API — delivering fast, high-quality responses. All requests go through HTTPS; no conversation data is stored.

A multi-layer safety system scans every message for crisis signals before the LLM responds, ensuring emergency resources are never delayed by AI reasoning.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

MindBridge is NOT a substitute for professional mental health care. It is not a licensed therapist, psychologist, or medical professional and cannot diagnose or treat any condition.

If you are in crisis, call 988 (US) or go to your nearest emergency room immediately.

🛠 Technology Stack

  • AI: openai/gpt-oss-120b (open-source) via Groq API
  • Backend: Python 3 + Flask
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript
  • Safety Layer: Keyword-based crisis detection + LLM risk prompting
  • Privacy: No conversation history stored server-side
