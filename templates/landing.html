About MindBridge

MindBridge is an open-source Agentic AI project built to make mental health support more accessible globally. It uses the openai/gpt-oss-120b language model running on Groq's ultra-fast inference API.

The project is designed with a defence-in-depth safety architecture — five independent layers ensure crisis resources reach users regardless of AI behaviour.

⚠️ Disclaimer: MindBridge is not a licensed therapist or medical professional. It cannot diagnose or treat any condition. Always consult a qualified mental health professional for diagnosis or treatment.
